WHEREAS, by Renewal and Extension Deed of Trust (the “Deed of Trust”) dated
November 9, 2023 and recorded on November 13, 2023 at Document Number 20230083176,
Official Records of El Paso County, Texas, BORDER PROPERTIES GROUP, LLC, a Texas
limited liability company (the “Grantor’’) as grantor conveyed to Mark Salloum as trustee certain
property situated in El Paso County, Texas to secure SM VER Enterprises, LLC, RAACK Capital,
LLC, BABK Trust 1 and KTDA Trust 1 (collectively, the “Lender”) as beneficiaries in the payment

of a Promissory Note dated November 9, 2023 in the original principal amount of $1,600,000.00 (the
“Note’’); and

WHEREAS, the Borrower defaulted on the Note, the maturity date of the Note was
accelerated and the entire balance is now due and owing in full, the Note remains unpaid and in
default, and the Lender desires to invoke the power of sale provided for in the Deed of Trust; and

WHEREAS, the Lender has appointed James W. Brewer as Substitute Trustee in the place
of the original trustee, the appointment being made in the manner authorized by the Deed of Trust.
Mr. Brewer’s contact information is James W. Brewer, Kemp Smith LLP, P.O. Box 2800, El Paso,
TX 79999-2800; james.brewer@kempsmith.com; (915) 533-4424; and

WHEREAS, the Lender has requested that the Substitute Trustee sell the Real Property
described and defined in the Deed of Trust.

NOW, THEREFORE, notice is hereby given that on May 5, 2026, beginning between
10:00 o’clock a.m. and 1:00 o’clock p.m., and concluding not later than 1:00 o’clock p.m. of that
day, Mr. Brewer as Substitute Trustee will sell the following property to the highest bidder for
cash. The sale will take place at the El Paso County Coliseum, 4100 E. Paisano Drive, El Paso,
Texas 79905, or at such other place as may be designated by the County Commissioners Court for
non-judicial foreclosure sales.

The real property to be sold is described on Exhibit “A” attached hereto (the “Real
Property”).

Lender has reserved the right to credit bid all or any portion of the indebtedness owing on
the Note.

Pursuant to Section 51.009 of the Texas Property Code, the Real Property will be sold “AS
IS”, without any expressed or implied warranties, except as to the warranties of title, and at the
purchaser’s own risk.

Pursuant to Section 51.0075(a) of the Texas Property Code, the Substitute Trustee reserves

the right to set further reasonable conditions for conducting the foreclosure sale. Any such further
conditions shall be announced before bidding is opened for the sale.
