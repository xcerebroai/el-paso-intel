hours thereafter.

79905, or at such other area as may be designated by the County
Commissioner's Court of El Paso County, Texas, or by the County
Commissioner's Office.

sale will be conducted as a public auction to the highest bidder for cash.

Deed of Trust, reserves the right to bid for the Property, and may bid as a credit all sums remaining due
to Lender under the Deed of Trust.

Pursuant to Section 51.009 of the Texas Property code, the Property will be sold “AS IS” without
any express or implied warranties. Prospective bidders are advised to conduct an independent
investigation of the nature, occupancy and physical condition of the Property.
